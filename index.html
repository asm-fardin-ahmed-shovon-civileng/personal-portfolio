About Me

Hi there! I'm A S M FARDIN AHMED SHOVON, an international undergraduate student in Civil Engineering at Nantong University, China, originally from Kurigram, Bangladesh. Now in my final year of undergraduate studies, I am developing my interests in structural engineering, sustainable construction, recycled concrete materials and innovative building technologies.

Through my ongoing research activities, I am exploring how construction and demolition waste can be transformed into valuable resources, how concrete durability can be enhanced and how innovative material solutions can reduce the environmental footprint of construction. I am particularly interested in bridging engineering research with practical solutions to the challenges of modern infrastructure and sustainable development.

To me, civil engineering goes beyond designing structures and building infrastructure. It is about rethinking the materials we use, the resources we consume and the legacy we leave for future generations. Welcome to my portfolio - a space where engineering knowledge meets research curiosity and a vision for a more sustainable built environment. Explore my academic journey, research interests and projects as I continue working toward that vision.
